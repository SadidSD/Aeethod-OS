### Announcement #16 | 2024-07-11T10:33:44.8650000+06:00 | By: **mtechcave**

Those of you with a Super Sorter reservation, I have made a new channel that may interest you: #super-sorter-mini 
Even if you don’t have a reservation, you can also utilize these products when they get released 🙂

We want to extend our gratitude, for those of you that ordered at least a deposit of a super sorter, we want to offer you to “borrow” one of the 4 bin models until your super sorter is in production, you can choose to buy and keep as well as receive the super sorter, send it back, or even before then, if you decide you like it more than the super sorter, you can have your deposit pay for the base unit: ($2999 value) and buy the modular upgrades to it 🙂

These machines will be release BEFORE the Super Sorter. Not because the Super Sorter is on the back burner but because the web interface is being built currently and we are doing it in phases.

First phase: the #simple-sifter : this machine is just a pi connected to the scanner that remotely uploads scans to the SortSwift software. This established the connection between the hardware and software. This will be released as a standalone module to be used manually with the scanner alone. This also creates “stop functions” for the scanner to stop at different criteria.

Second phase: the #deleted-channel : this is a binary sorting system that establishes movement based criteria based on sorting methods provided by the user, pass or reject type system. Then left to one bin, turn right to another bin. This expands on the stop criteria as well as introduces early stages to smaller based stores or users. This connects into the simple sifter via usb.

Third phase: the #super-sorter-mini this is a modular system with one central conveyor that works similarly to the simple splitter, however it introduces the tilt functions of the super sorter. This also has screens on each bin and the continue, stop, and notification screens of the super sorter. This machine will start with 4 bins and will range up with modularity to 6, possibly even 8 bins, all with the same central module. This introduces the tilt function and screen controls of the super sorter, as well as one card detection sensor. This does not require the simple sifter.

Final phase: the #super-sorter this is the machine we all know and love. This machine will have 28+1 bins and if I can visualize right may end up with 30 bins. This introduces movement features, 14 detection sensors, fans, and auto feed hopper. 

The reason we want to introduce these other items is because we will already have (and do already have most of it done in software) the code to do it, so why not release it as products that can improve our software usability right now. If you guys have opinions on this, let me know, we want to give back to you guys for being so patient with us, and give you something you can use while you wait on the best of all sorters to be complete. Each of these machines should run as fast as the scanner can run, we are hoping to operate them at about 1500-2000 cards per hour, as they do not have the buffer conveyor to “queue” card scans for recognition. They would also need to be hand filled as they do not have a hopper like the super sorter.

### Announcement #22 | 2024-08-11T00:48:35.5150000+06:00 | By: **dazzah02**

@everyone **🚨 Introducing *Simple Sifter* – the ultimate tool for TCG Card Scanning!** 🎉

----------------------------------

### 🔍 **What is Simple Sifter?**

**Simple Sifter** is powerful software for **Raspberry Pi** (models 3 and 4) that gives you **full control** of your scanner, integrating seamlessly with our recognition engine. Whether cataloguing your collection or listing cards, Simple Sifter streamlines your workflow, harnessing the **Fujitsu 8170**'s speed and power.

----------------------------------

### ✨ **Key Features**

🌐 **Remote Control**  
*Control your scanner from anywhere*, ensuring accurate, high-quality scans.

🛰️ **Direct Streaming**  
*Stream scanned TCG cards directly to the SortSwift web interface*, no manual uploads or saving of files needed.

⚡ **Efficient Workflow**  
*Automate and optimize your scanning process*, so you can focus on your collection.

🔃 **Automatic Updates**  
*Simple Sifter automatically updates*, ensuring you always have the latest features. Future updates will include advanced features like automatic stop criteria, enhancing efficiency.

----------------------------------

### 🚀 **Installation Guide**

1. **Download** the Simple Sifter image from our site.
2. Use **Balena Etcher** to write the image to an SD card.
3. Insert the SD card into your Raspberry Pi.
4. Connect to the `simplesifter-xxx` Wi-Fi network.
5. Follow on-screen instructions to connect to your Wi-Fi.
6. Connect your scanner to the Raspberry Pi.
7. Ensure both the scanner and Raspberry Pi are powered on.

----------------------------------

### 🔗 **[Download Now](https://sortswift.com/SimpleSifter)**

----------------------------------

### ⚠️ **Note:**  
*A scanning subscription with [SortSwift](https://sortswift.com) is required to use this software.*

A more in-depth guide/tutorial will come on Monday.

Preassembled Kits will be available in the not so distant future with a some cool additional extras and features!

- Create ascending, non-overlapping tiers (e.g., $0-$4.99, $5-$19.99, $20+)
- **Each range has its own step order, modifiers, and rounding**
- Smart range escape if modifiers push price outside its range

## Price Sources

- TCGPlayer (USD), CardMarket (EUR), CardTrader (USD/EUR), ManaPool (USD)
- Four slots: Primary, Fallback, Final Fallback, Doomsday
- Modes: Follow Fallbacks, Use Highest, Use Lowest, Use Average
- Never forces $0

## Rounding (Per Range)

Each range has its own rounding:
- **Round-up only** ($1.01 with $0.25 becomes $1.25)
- Increments: **$0.01, $0.10, $0.25, $0.49, $0.50, $0.95, $0.99, $1.00**
- **Skip rounding when rarity baseline applied** (optional)
- Examples: $8.39 with .49 becomes $8.49. $1.50 with .99 becomes $1.99

## Modifiers

- **Condition**: NM, LP, MP, HP, DM percentage curves
- **Language/Printing**: Game-aware adjustments
- **Rarity modifiers and baselines**: Per-printing in advanced mode
- **Age modifiers**: Ranges like 1-5 days, 6-10 days
- **Release window**: Rules based on set release date
- **Fixed/Percentage offsets**: Supports negative values
- **Temporary offsets**: With expiration date/time
- **Max price movement**: Cap increase/decrease from current price

## Per-Item and Set Controls

- **Price Override/Floor**: Lock price or set minimum per item
- **Set Overrides**: Override price points and modifiers for specific sets
- All sync across matching SKUs

## Per-Item Floor

A dedicated step allowing individual SKUs to define their own minimum price while still floating with the market.

Example: Sealed Spiderman boxes with a per-item floor of **$399** and a market price around **$599** will:
- Move freely with all pricing steps
- Increase or decrease with market shifts
- **Never drop below $399** regardless of modifiers, offsets, or source price behavior

Per-item floors run after modifiers and offsets but before rounding (configurable), ensuring protection without blocking upward movement.

- Search any card and see all price points from every source
- View complete step-by-step breakdown of final price
- Compare all printings side by side with synchronized steps
- Mock override, floor, and current price for testing

1. Enable V2 in navbar
2. Click **Autopricing** in sidebar
3. Set marketplace sources and price points
4. Add price ranges and configure step order
5. Use **Test with Sample Card** to verify
6. Save and reprice

In SortSwift this is under Help & Useful > Condition Grading. Press **Start**, then for each card:
1. **Look at both sides.** Cards are scanned on a black background, so the edge shows as a thin white line. An even line is clean. Where it gets thicker or breaks into specks, that is wear. Press **F** to flip front and back, **C** for contrast, and zoom in on the corners.
2. **Grade it.** If the card shows the machine's grade, press **Looks right** (Enter) or **Not right** (D) and pick the right grade. Otherwise pick a grade with **1 to 5** (NM, LP, MP, HP, DMG). Your corrections are what train it most.
3. **Paint the wear.** Use the whitening brush (**W**) to mark where the wear is. If a face has none, press **N** for "No wear on this face". The spots you mark teach the model *where* wear is, not just the grade.
4. **Can't tell?** Press **U** for Can't grade (a bad scan, for example) or **S** to skip.

Press **G** any time for the grading guide and **?** for every shortcut.
